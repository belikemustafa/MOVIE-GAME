❓ How to Play CineSpotle

Identify the secret movie in 10 guesses or fewer. After each guess, every attribute tile flips to show how close you are:

✓ Green (Exact Match): This attribute matches the secret movie exactly.
≈ Gold (Close / Partial Match):
  • Genres: Shares at least 1 genre.
  • Year: Within ±5 years of the target film.
  • Box Office: Within ±25% of worldwide gross.
  • Score: Within ±7 points out of 100.
  • Rated: Adjacent age rating (e.g., U & UA, PG & PG-13).
  • Studio / Music: Sister studio or shared composer.
  • Cast: Actor appears in a different role (Lead ↔ Support).
↑ / ↓ Directional Arrows: ↑ means the mystery film is newer, higher grossing, or higher rated. ↓ means older or lower.
