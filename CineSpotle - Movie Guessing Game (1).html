How to Play CineSpotle

Guess the mystery movie in 10 tries or fewer. Each guess reveals detailed clues comparing your movie to the secret target film:

GREEN Exact match for Genre, Year, Box Office, Score, Rating, Studio, Music Composer, or Cast/Director role!
GOLD Close match: Partial genre overlap, Year within 5 yrs, Score within 5 pts, close Box Office, sister Studio, or a Cast/Director credited in a different role in the target film.
↑ / ↓ ↑ means the mystery movie's Year, Box Office, or Score is higher than your guess; ↓ means lower.

Use the top filter bar to play across All Cinema or focus specifically on Hollywood, Bollywood, Kannada, or Pan-India movies!
